凸 Kakeya 集合Convex Kakeya Sets

持续探索中Ongoing Exploration
凸几何Convex Geometry Kakeya 问题Kakeya Problem 旋转Rotations 几何优化Geometric Optimization

我正在探索 Kakeya 问题的一个凸几何变体：一个集合必须包含给定平面集合 \(S\) 的所有连续平移旋转副本。我感兴趣的是面积、直径和周长等几何量，以及最优构型的稳定性与极限行为。

I am exploring a convex-geometric variant of the Kakeya problem in which a set must contain continuously translated rotated copies of a prescribed planar set \(S\). I am interested in geometric quantities such as area, diameter and perimeter, as well as stability and limiting behavior of optimal configurations.

球面上的 Poisson 方程Poisson Equation on the Sphere

探索 · 阐述性项目Exploration · Expository Project
椭圆 PDEElliptic PDE 球面分析Spherical Analysis 正则性Regularity

我正在研究球面上的 Poisson 方程， \[\Delta_{S^2} u = f,\] 目标是尽可能纯粹地从分析的角度理解它。

I am working through the Poisson equation on the sphere, \[\Delta_{S^2} u = f,\] with the aim of understanding it as purely analytically as possible.

  • 弱形式
  • weak formulation
  • Lax–Milgram 定理
  • Lax–Milgram theorem
  • Poincaré 不等式
  • Poincaré inequality
  • \(H^1\) 估计
  • \(H^1\) estimates
  • 正则性
  • regularity
  • Moser 迭代
  • Moser iteration
  • Green 函数
  • Green functions

主要目标是先理解其分析结构，再引入谱分解——之后再引入球谐函数与谱理论作为另一种观点。

The main goal is to understand the analytic structure first, before introducing the spectral decomposition — later bringing in spherical harmonics and spectral theory as another point of view.
