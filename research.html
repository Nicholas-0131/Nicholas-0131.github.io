Convex Geometry & Kakeya-type Problems

凸几何与 Kakeya 型问题

Convex Geometry Kakeya Problem Rotations Geometric Optimization

A direction I am currently exploring. Consider a planar set \(S \subset \mathbb{R}^2\) and a continuous translation function \(x : [0, 2\pi) \to \mathbb{R}^2\). I am interested in a set \(A\) that contains all the rotated translated copies \[ R_\theta S + x(\theta). \]

Questions I am currently thinking about include:

  • minimal possible area
  • diameter
  • perimeter
  • geometric structure of optimal configurations
  • dependence on the original set \(S\)
  • behavior under convergence of \(S\)
  • the transition between line-segment-type configurations and rotationally symmetric configurations

Convex Kakeya Sets

Ongoing Exploration

04

Poisson Equation on the Sphere

Exploration · Expository Project
Elliptic PDESpherical AnalysisRegularity

I am working through the Poisson equation on the sphere, \[\Delta_{S^2} u = f,\] with the aim of understanding it as purely analytically as possible.

  • weak formulation
  • Lax–Milgram theorem
  • Poincaré inequality
  • \(H^1\) estimates
  • regularity
  • Moser iteration
  • Green functions

The main goal is to understand the analytic structure first, before introducing the spectral decomposition — later bringing in spherical harmonics and spectral theory as another point of view.
