我的第一个博客！

早上好，或者说，大家好！

An introductory post: as a first-year undergraduate I am interested in differential geometry and plan to focus on geometric analysis. I am reading Evans's Partial Differential Equations and the Gidas–Spruck paper, and will write down my reading notes from time to time.
